About Ajay Satya Prakash Public School

Ajay Satya Prakash Public School (ASPPS) is a co-educational private school located in Panagar (Gram Keolari), Jabalpur, Madhya Pradesh. Established in 2012 by Mr. Ajay Grover, our school offers CBSE curriculum from Preschool & Nursery through Higher Secondary (Class XII), bringing together academics, sports, arts, and modern skills for holistic development.
